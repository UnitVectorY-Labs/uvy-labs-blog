The content on this blog covers open-source projects published under **[github.com/UnitVectorY-Labs](https://github.com/UnitVectorY-Labs)** and is primarily generated automatically by AI from repository releases and commit history.

The goal is both to share practical updates about each project and to demonstrate how language models can research release changes and draft structured technical announcements. Every article includes a transparency note identifying the model, source repository, release tag, and generation date.

The pipeline that researches releases and authors these articles is open source in **[release-storyteller](https://github.com/UnitVectorY-Labs/release-storyteller)**.
